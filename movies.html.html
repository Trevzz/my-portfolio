My Top 5 Movies

Five films I can watch again and again, ranked from favourite down.

#1Entergalactic (2022)

AnimationMusicRomance

A beautifully animated New York love story from Kid Cudi, with a soundtrack that fits perfectly with my music taste. The tagline says it all: Love will find us all.

#2Fast Five (2011)

ActionHeistCars

The franchise turned into a heist film, with a loyal crew, big stunts and the Rio vault chase. It is pure teamwork and adrenaline.

#3Rocky IV (1985)

BoxingSportClassic

The training montages and the never-give-up spirit feed my love of combat sports. It is the film that makes me want to push harder.

#4Project X (2012)

ComedyParty

Total chaos from start to finish and one of the funniest party films ever made. It is my go-to movie night pick with friends.

#5Captain America: The Winter Soldier (2014)

MarvelSpy thrillerAction

A superhero film that plays like a political spy thriller. The fight scenes are sharp, and the story about trust and freedom stays with me.
