About Me

I am a second-year BSc Environmental Science student at Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT), studying with first-class honours. I love fieldwork: during my attachment with the Kenya Forest Service I carried out forest inventories, monitored soil and the environment, and led environmental education workshops for communities.

I turn data into clear stories using Microsoft PowerPoint, Word and Excel, and I am now adding web design to that toolkit. I am looking for an internship where I can apply my training and contribute to an organisation's day-to-day work.

Fun fact: I can go from identifying pollution sources in the field to debating wrestling entrances for hours.
